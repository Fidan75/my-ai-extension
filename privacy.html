Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Sumora AI ("we", "our", or "the Service") provides an AI-powered Chrome extension that helps users summarize text. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use Sumora AI.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process:

  • Account information, including your email address, through our authentication provider.
  • Text that you select and submit for summarization.
  • Account identifiers used to associate usage with your account.
  • Usage information, including the date and number of summaries requested.
  • Subscription and transaction information handled by our payment provider if paid subscriptions are enabled.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the Service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide AI-powered text summarization.
  • Manage user accounts and authentication.
  • Track daily usage limits.
  • Manage subscriptions if paid features are available.
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the Service.
  • Respond to customer support requests.

3. Text Submitted for Summarization

The extension retrieves text selected by the user on the active webpage only after the user clicks the "Summarize Text" button. It does not automatically retrieve the entire webpage for summarization.

When a summarization request is submitted, the selected text is transmitted to our backend and sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate a summary.

Our backend's daily usage database stores account identifiers, usage dates, and summary counts. It does not intentionally store submitted text in that database.

Google processes submitted text according to its applicable terms, privacy policies, and data-processing practices. Third-party providers may have their own data retention practices.

Please do not submit passwords, confidential information, or sensitive personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

Sumora AI uses or may use the following services:

  • Supabase: Account authentication and database services.
  • Google Gemini: AI processing and text summarization.
  • Render: Backend hosting.
  • Paddle: Payment processing if subscriptions are enabled.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Our daily usage database stores account identifiers, usage dates, and summary counts for usage-limit management.

Account information may be retained by our authentication provider while your account remains active.

Submitted text may also be processed by our backend and third-party AI provider to generate summaries. Other information may be processed or retained by our service providers according to their respective policies and retention practices.

6. Data Security

We use technical measures intended to protect information processed through the Service. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

To make a privacy-related request, including an account deletion request, contact us at the email address below. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and operational purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Sumora AI is not intended for children below the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our Service or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact: sumoraaisupport@gmail.com
