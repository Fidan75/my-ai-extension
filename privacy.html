Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Sumora AI ("we", "our", or "the Service") provides an AI-powered text summarization Chrome extension. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use Sumora AI.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the Service, information may include:

  • Account information, such as your email address.
  • Text you select and submit for summarization.
  • Usage information, such as the number of summaries requested.
  • Subscription and transaction information handled by our payment provider.
  • Technical information required to operate and secure the Service.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Provide AI text summarization.
  • Manage user accounts and authentication.
  • Track usage limits and subscription status.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.
  • Respond to support requests.

3. Text Submitted for Summarization

Text submitted through Sumora AI is sent to our backend for processing by an AI service provider. Do not submit passwords, confidential information, or sensitive personal information.

Details about storage and retention of submitted text must be confirmed against the current service implementation and third-party provider policies.

4. Third-Party Services

Sumora AI may use third-party services to operate its features, including:

  • Supabase for authentication and database services.
  • Google Gemini for AI summarization.
  • Render for backend hosting.
  • Paddle for payment processing, if subscriptions are enabled.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

5. Data Security

We use technical measures intended to protect information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, subject to applicable legal requirements. Specific retention periods depend on the type of information and the services used.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us to make a privacy-related request.

8. Children's Privacy

Sumora AI is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our Service or practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
