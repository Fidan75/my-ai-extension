Refund Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refund requests for paid Sumora AI subscriptions are handled.

1. Subscription Payments

If paid subscriptions become available, payment details and subscription terms will be displayed before checkout.

2. Refund Requests

If you believe you have been charged incorrectly, or experience a billing issue, contact us with your account email and relevant transaction details.

3. Review of Requests

Refund requests will be reviewed individually, taking into account the circumstances of the purchase and applicable consumer protection laws.

Nothing in this policy limits any mandatory refund or cancellation rights you may have under applicable law.

4. Subscription Cancellation

If recurring subscriptions are offered, cancellation instructions will be provided through the applicable subscription or payment management process. Cancelling a subscription generally prevents future renewals; it does not automatically mean a refund for a previous payment.

5. Contact Us

To request a refund review or ask a billing question, email: sumoraaisupport@gmail.com
