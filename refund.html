Refund Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refund requests for paid Sumora AI subscriptions are handled.

1. Subscription Payments

If paid subscriptions become available, payment details, subscription prices, billing periods, and applicable terms will be displayed before checkout.

Payment processing and subscription management may be provided by Paddle or another designated payment provider.

2. Refund Requests

If you believe you have been charged incorrectly or experience a billing issue, contact us at our support email address.

Please include your account email and relevant transaction reference so that we can identify the payment. Never send your password or full payment card details.

3. Review of Requests

Refund requests will be reviewed individually, taking into account the circumstances of the purchase, the applicable payment provider's procedures, and applicable consumer protection laws.

Nothing in this policy limits any mandatory refund, cancellation, or other consumer rights you may have under applicable law.

4. Subscription Cancellation

If recurring subscriptions are offered, cancellation instructions will be provided through the applicable subscription or payment management process.

Cancelling a subscription generally prevents future renewals. Cancellation does not automatically mean that a refund will be issued for a previous payment.

5. Contact Us

To request a refund review or ask a billing question, email: sumoraaisupport@gmail.com
